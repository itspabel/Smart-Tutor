Features

Everything a tutor needs, in one app

Replace scattered paper registers and messy spreadsheets. Smart Tutor keeps your students, fees, attendance and exams organized — offline, on your device.

Batch & Student Management

Organize students by batch, class or subject. Store contact and parent details, admission dates, monthly fees and custom notes — with one-tap call and quick search.

Accurate Fee Tracking

Upcoming, Due and Overdue statuses are calculated from each student's joining date and payment deadline — never mark current-month fees overdue by mistake.

Partial & Advance Payments

Track partial payments with exact remaining balances, and record multi-month advance payments with custom month chips.

Digital Receipts & Due List

Generate a digital receipt and share it instantly via WhatsApp or SMS. The dedicated Due List and Fee Summary dashboard makes follow-ups quick.

Class Scheduling & Reminders

Manage weekly routines, extra classes, make-up sessions and holidays. Get a reminder notification when your scheduled batch time begins.

Attendance Registers

Swift roll call with Present, Absent, Late or Excused in a single tap. See monthly attendance matrices and percentage stats per student.

Teaching Analytics

Review total hours taught, regular class days and extra classes conducted — a clear picture of your teaching effort.

Exams & Report Cards

Create class tests, term exams and subject assessments. Record marks, calculate percentages and grades automatically, then share a student report card with parents.

Expense & Income Tracker

Log teaching-related expenses like books, stationery, photocopies and conveyance. Monthly cashflow summaries show your net tutor earnings.

Biometric & PIN Lock

Keep confidential student records secure with biometric fingerprint or PIN app lock.

JSON Backup & Restore

Complete JSON backup and restore lets you transfer your data across devices. Backups are generated locally — you control the file.

Offline-First & Lightweight

Smart Tutor runs 100% offline with no account required. The APK is about 3.2 MB with no bloatware or background battery drain.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Smart Tutor?

Smart Tutor is an all-in-one management app for private tutors, coaching centre instructors, home educators and academic mentors. It manages student profiles, batches, attendance, fee dues, class schedules, exams and teaching expenses in one Android app.

Is Smart Tutor available on Google Play?

No. Smart Tutor is distributed as an Android APK through GitHub Releases. The release page is the official download destination.

How do I download and install it?

Open the latest release, download the APK asset, then open the downloaded file and follow Android's installation prompts. If Android requests permission to install unknown apps, allow it for the relevant installation source only if you trust the APK.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Smart Tutor is designed as an offline-first app. Your student data stays strictly on your device and an online account is not required.

Where is my student data stored?

Student and tuition records are stored locally on your device. Smart Tutor does not require an online account, and the app includes a biometric fingerprint or PIN app lock.

How do I back up or restore my data?

Smart Tutor supports complete JSON backup and restore, which can be used to transfer data across devices. Backups are generated locally, so keep the backup file somewhere you trust.

How large is the APK?

Smart Tutor version 1.6 ships as ABI-specific release builds for arm64-v8a, armeabi-v7a and x86_64. The APK is approximately 3.2 MB, with no bloatware or background battery drain.

How can I report a bug?

Please open an issue on the GitHub issue tracker. Including your device model, Android version and steps to reproduce helps a lot.
